# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis Using SQL and Tableau

## Overview
This project analyzes Superstore sales data to identify revenue trends, profit drivers, discount risks, and underperforming product areas. The analysis was completed using SQL inside a Kaggle notebook, Python for data preparation and visualization, and Tableau for the final interactive dashboard.

## Business Questions
- Which regions generate the most sales and profit?
- Which product categories and sub-categories are most and least profitable?
- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue and profit?
- How do discounts affect profit margin?
- What recommendations can improve profitability?

## Tools Used
- SQL
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- SQLite
- Tableau

## Key Findings
- The business generated approximately $2.30M in sales and $286.4K in profit.
- The overall profit margin was 12.47%.
- The West region generated the highest total profit.
- Technology was the most profitable category.
- Furniture had weak profitability despite high sales.
- Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies were unprofitable sub-categories.
- Discounts above 20% were associated with negative profitability.
- Some high-sales products generated negative profit.

## Recommendations
- Review and reduce high discounts, especially discounts above 20%.
- Investigate weak Furniture sub-categories such as Tables and Bookcases.
- Promote stronger profit areas such as Technology, Office Supplies, Copiers, Paper, and Accessories.
- Review regional differences to understand why the West and East regions outperform the Central region.
- Evaluate products using sales, profit, and profit margin together instead of sales alone.
